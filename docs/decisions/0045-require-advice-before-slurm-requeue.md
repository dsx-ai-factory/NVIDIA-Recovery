# ADR 0045: Require Advice Before Slurm Requeue

Status: accepted

Date: 2026-10-01

> **Implementation note (2026-10-06):** [Public Helm defaults](../../deploy/helm/slurm-advisor-controller/values.yaml)
> at `9a30d875` now disable eager recovery; explicit compatibility opt-in retains
> the bounded retries and phase budget from ADRs 0043 and 0044 and emits a warning.

This decision supersedes the direct failed-job recovery and associated
recovery-decision retention rules in
[ADR 0022](0022-bootstrap-sac-job-discovery-once.md#retain-failure-work-until-its-recovery-decision-completes).
Its bootstrap, fresh-observation, and Advice-discovery rules remain in force.
[ADR 0010](0010-resolve-squeue-final-state-during-completing.md) continues to
govern early failure detection during COMPLETING; with eager recovery disabled,
observing a failure does not by itself authorize requeue.

This decision also supersedes
[ADR 0013](0013-correlate-recovery-with-durable-trace-context.md#implementation-order-decision)’s
requirement to complete tracing compatibility for direct failed-job recovery
before converging recovery behind Advice. Its durable sequence identity,
per-Advice context, and applicable recovery telemetry requirements remain in
force.

## Context

The Slurm Advisor Controller (SAC) currently calls `reconcileFailedJobs()`
after publishing job observations and before reconciling Advice. It can issue
`requeuehold` for a failed attempt N without first receiving `RESTARTED(N)`.
This eager recovery preserves a scheduler record while backend analysis and
Advice production proceed, reducing the risk of Slurm purging the final job
record after its `MinJobAge` retention period before recovery can be requested.

The direct path checks the job-level retry limit, while Job Manager evaluates
the effective system/job retry limit and checkpoint-progress stopping. SAC can
therefore create held N+1 before Job Manager publishes `TERMINATED(N)`.
Termination targets the exact source attempt and becomes stale once N+1 is
observed. The watchdog can subsequently release an eligible held successor,
allowing recovery to continue despite the backend's stop decision.

Performance optimizations have largely mitigated the polling-latency issues
that motivated eager recovery. Relevant architectural changes include
[bounded publication and accounting concurrency](0020-bound-slurm-controller-publication-and-accounting-concurrency.md),
[one-time backend discovery](0022-bootstrap-sac-job-discovery-once.md), and
[asynchronous active log-metadata enrichment](0035-enrich-active-slurm-log-metadata-asynchronously.md).
This is the basis for accepting the remaining delay, not a guarantee that all
backend or scheduler operations finish within the retention window.

The selected priority is to keep recovery expressed through the existing
Advice model rather than introduce a second recovery-policy layer in SAC.

## Decision

### Require Advice when eager recovery is disabled

With eager recovery disabled, SAC must receive applicable `RESTARTED` Advice
before it creates a successor attempt. The existing eager recovery path may
remain available for rollout and rollback. Enabling it retains the policy
race described above; backend delays must not automatically enable it. This
compatibility path does not introduce a new policy layer.

Continue detecting and publishing failures promptly, including during
COMPLETING. With eager recovery disabled, early observation can start backend
analysis sooner, but scheduler recovery waits for Advice. With the existing
phase ordering, Advice generated by a job update normally becomes available to
SAC on a subsequent tick.
This decision requires no synchronous eligibility query or additional Advice
states to bypass that ordering.

### Preserve the existing division of responsibility

Job Manager retains recovery-policy evaluation and Advice production,
including the effective system/job retry limit, checkpoint stopping, completion
policy, and existing attribution processing. Keep its `TERMINATED` publication,
recovery notifications, and DAG-abort handling under
[ADR 0011](0011-cancel-dag-dependents-after-parent-abort.md) and
[ADR 0040](0040-retire-job-anomaly-relay.md).

SAC retains scheduler-specific execution and applicability checks: ownership,
environment and partition scope, operating mode, action acknowledgement,
attempt identity, and fresh scheduler state. Advice remains subject to those
checks; removing eager recovery does not require executing an unsafe or stale
recommendation.

Do not add policy configuration polling, effective-policy or checkpoint-summary
fields to job responses, materialized checkpoint-progress counters, or durable
eligibility state in SAC to implement this decision. Those were alternatives
to waiting for Advice, not prerequisites for recovery through Advice.

### Preserve reconciliation and observation contracts

`RESTARTED(N)` continues to apply to source N and successor N+1. SAC skips
requeue when fresh Slurm state already shows the recovery transition, then
completes applicable node constraints and release. `TERMINATED(N)` remains
exact-attempt intent: an already-final N satisfies it without cancellation;
it is not retargeted to a successor. Disabling eager recovery prevents the
direct path from creating a successor ahead of the backend decision; it does
not redefine manual requeues or promise atomicity between all producers and
Slurm.

Keep generation-scoped convergence and abandonment under
[ADR 0025](0025-filter-and-terminally-reconcile-actionable-advice.md) and
[ADR 0026](0026-classify-advice-expiration-in-scheduler-controller.md).
Command success alone is not convergence. Missing Advice or a transient
backend failure does not authorize fallback recovery or introduce a new
policy-denial terminal status.

If the direct recovery path is removed entirely, also remove seen-attempt
retention and deduplication used only for retrying its mutations. If it remains
behind a feature flag, retain the bookkeeping needed when that flag is enabled.
Preserve observation and publication retries,
including final accounting after an active COMPLETING attempt disappears.
Durable Advice remains a discovery source for subsequent scheduler work; a
final observation must not be discarded before successful publication.

The watchdog retains its existing role and eligibility rules under
[ADR 0009](0009-derive-watchdog-release-from-slurm-state.md) and
[ADR 0042](0042-align-sac-and-watchdog-job-action-policy.md). It releases
eligible existing holds; it does not create successor attempts. This decision
does not add a watchdog dependency on Advice or new eligibility markers.

## Consequences

With eager recovery disabled, recovery has one initiating recommendation path.
A failed observation alone cannot advance N to N+1 before retry and checkpoint
policy is reflected in Advice. The job can remain failed when Job Manager
decides to stop, preserving existing termination, notification, and DAG behavior
without canceling an unnecessary successor.

Recovery now depends on the time required to produce and consume applicable
Advice. Backend unavailability, attribution delay, or polling backlog can
still outlast Slurm's retention period and prevent requeue. We accept that
availability tradeoff; performance improvements reduce the risk but do not
remove it. A recovery that no longer fits the retention window is an
operational failure to diagnose, not permission to silently reactivate eager
requeue.

Implementation must gate the direct action path and its bookkeeping and
telemetry consistently. Retain useful observation and Advice telemetry. Verification with eager recovery disabled
must show that a failed or failing-COMPLETING job is published without requeue
in the absence of Advice, `TERMINATED(N)`
leaves a failed source final without creating a successor, and applicable
`RESTARTED(N)` still completes recovery without duplicate requeue. Cover
publication retries, delayed Advice discovery, observe mode, and the existing
watchdog behavior through production interfaces. If a feature flag is retained,
verify its default and explicit opt-in behavior. Scheduler-action changes
require the controller harness and human review.

The [current architecture](../architecture/slurm-advisor-controller.md#wait-for-recovery-advice)
documents deployment configuration and defaults.
[Controller regression tests](../../slurm-advisor-controller/internal/reconcile/advice_recovery_test.go)
cover publication retries, delayed Advice, and observed convergence.

## Alternatives Rejected

- **Keep eager requeue and replicate policy in SAC.** Requires consistent
  system/job limit resolution and checkpoint evidence on every requeue path,
  adding a policy layer and backend consistency concerns to the adapter.
- **Return effective policy or an eligibility decision from job upserts.**
  Avoids waiting for a later Advice poll but introduces a second recovery
  contract alongside Advice; checkpoint aggregation and failure semantics
  would also need to be defined.
- **Keep speculative successors and cancel them after a late veto.** Changes
  exact-attempt termination semantics or introduces recovery-sequence tracking;
  watchdog release and further failures can advance beyond N+1 before rejection.
- **Treat a held successor as the prior attempt.** Conflates execution identity
  with hold state, does not cover a released or immediately schedulable successor,
  and still creates an attempt that policy may reject.
